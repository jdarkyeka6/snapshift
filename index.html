How to play SnapShift

  1. Drag a shape close to a spot and magnetic snap will pull it to the nearest valid position. Or tap a shape then the board.
  2. Fill a full row or column to blast it away and earn points.
  3. Clear lines on consecutive moves for a bigger combo multiplier.
  4. When all three blocks are placed, three new ones appear. Keep playing until none of your blocks fit!
